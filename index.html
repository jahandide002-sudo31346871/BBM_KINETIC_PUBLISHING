BAZI BA MARG — DEATH IS NOT THE END

Online Combat Racing IP

A brutal post-apocalyptic combat-racing game built around high-speed racing, tactical vehicle combat, destruction, survival and competitive elimination.

Quick Pitch

Players drive heavily armored combat vehicles through dangerous industrial tracks while using machine guns, missiles, ramming and boost systems to fight rivals, survive and qualify for the next stage.

Core Experience

High-speed combat racing • weaponized vehicles • armor and destruction • dangerous post-apocalyptic environments • competitive elimination • online multiplayer • spectator participation.

Vehicle Vision

10 distinct combat vehicles are planned. HUNTER is the initial flagship heavy vehicle, with roles including REAPER, RAVEN and BULLDOZER.

IRON CANYON

Planned 2.5 km industrial post-apocalyptic track with five sectors: Industrial Gate, Canyon Bend, Bridge Run, Refinery Tunnel and Death Basin.

Production Foundation

Documented production foundation covering vehicle architecture, combat, racing AI, track design, multiplayer, spectator systems, progression, UI/HUD, backend, tournament design and Unreal Engine 5 planning.

Current stage: concept + production foundation / vertical-slice development.
Playable demo: Not currently available.

Financial & Production Roadmap

  1. Vertical Slice — HUNTER, combat, AI, IRON CANYON and HUD.
  2. Pre-production — roster, online architecture, art pipeline and team.
  3. Full Production — vehicles, tracks, multiplayer, progression, destruction, audio/VFX.
  4. QA / Certification / Launch.

Budget and final schedule to be scoped jointly with the selected publishing/development partner.

What We Are Seeking

Publishing and funding • development guidance • marketing/PR • technical/operational support • co-development • commercial structures including potential IP acquisition, subject to negotiation.
